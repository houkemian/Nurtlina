Support

English / 中文

Contact Nurtlina
support@nurtlina.app

For faster help

Include the app version, Android version, device model, and a short description of what happened. Do not send baby names, care notes, passwords, purchase tokens, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Purchases

For subscription management, use Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. For missing Pro access, first use Restore purchases in Nurtlina, then contact support with the product name and approximate purchase date—never full payment details.

Privacy requests

Use the subject “Privacy request” and state whether you are requesting access, correction, export, or deletion. Account deletion instructions are on the data deletion page.

支持

联系邮箱：support@nurtlina.app。

为了更快处理

请提供应用版本、Android 版本、设备型号及问题简述。请勿发送宝宝姓名、照护备注、密码、购买令牌或其他不必要的敏感信息。

购买问题

请在 Google Play → 付款和订阅中管理订阅。Pro 权益缺失时，请先在 Nurtlina 使用“恢复购买”，再向客服提供商品名称和大致购买日期；请勿提供完整付款资料。

隐私请求

邮件主题请写“Privacy request”，并说明需要访问、更正、导出还是删除。账号删除步骤参见数据删除页面。
