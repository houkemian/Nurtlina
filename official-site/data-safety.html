Key practices

  • Core care logging works locally and does not require the network.
  • Data is encrypted in transit when sent to connected services.
  • Baby names and note text are not sent as analytics events.
  • Synced baby care records are not used for advertising targeting.
  • Account deletion is available in-app and through an external request.
  • Nurtlina requests no location, contacts, camera, or microphone permission.

User controls

You can deny notification permission, disable reminders, sign out, delete individual records, export supported records, or delete your account and associated data. See the Privacy Policy for details.
