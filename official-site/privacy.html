Nurtlina: Baby Feeding Tracker

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 18 August 2026

查看中文

This policy explains how Nurtlina (“Nurtlina”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the Nurtlina Android app, website, and related services. Contact us at support@nurtlina.app.

1. Who the app is for

Nurtlina is intended for adult parents and caregivers. It is not directed to children and is not a medical service. We encourage caregivers to use nicknames rather than a baby’s legal name.

2. Information handled

Care records you enter

Baby nickname, optional birth date, feeding records, diaper records, sleep records, reminder feedback, settings, and optional notes. Core records are stored locally on your device. If you sign in and use backup or sync, selected records and sync metadata are transmitted to our backend.

Account and authentication

If you sign in with Google, we may receive an account identifier, email address, and display name needed to authenticate you, associate your account with a family workspace, provide backup and sync, and support account deletion.

Purchases and entitlements

Google Play and RevenueCat process purchases. We receive product, transaction, entitlement, and app-user identifiers needed to provide and restore Pro access. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

Analytics, diagnostics, and advertising

Firebase Analytics may receive app interaction events such as onboarding, feature use, whether a feeding amount was entered, reminder interaction, purchase status, language, and widget use. Firebase Crashlytics may receive crash traces and device/app diagnostics. Google AdMob may process device identifiers, IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver and measure banner ads. We do not send baby names, exact feeding amounts, or note contents as analytics events.

Device data

The app may process notification permission and device time locally to schedule reminders. Depending on your Android and Google backup settings, Android may include app data in device backup or transfer.

3. Why we use information

  • Provide local logging, summaries, reminders, widgets, export, backup, and sync.
  • Authenticate accounts and protect access to cloud records.
  • Provide, verify, and restore purchases.
  • Maintain reliability, prevent abuse, and diagnose failures.
  • Measure privacy-safe product usage and display ads to eligible Free users.
  • Respond to support, privacy, export, and deletion requests.

4. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell baby care records. We use service providers only as needed to operate the app, including Google Firebase (authentication, analytics, crash reporting and related cloud services), Google Play (distribution and billing), Google AdMob (advertising), RevenueCat (purchase entitlement management), and infrastructure providers that host our API and database. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy notices. We may disclose information when legally required or to protect users, the service, or others.

5. Advertising and consent

Free users may see a Today-page banner; Pro users and night mode do not. Where required, we will request applicable advertising or analytics choices. Device and Google settings may provide additional controls. Baby care records are not used for advertising targeting.

6. Storage, security, and international processing

Core care records remain usable offline. Cloud transmission uses encrypted network connections. Access to synced records is scoped to authenticated accounts or families. No system is completely secure. Service providers may process data in countries other than your own, subject to applicable safeguards.

7. Retention

Local data remains on your device until you delete it, clear app data, or uninstall the app, subject to Android backup behavior. Cloud account and care data is retained while your account is active. When deletion is requested, identifying account fields are removed and associated cloud records are deleted or marked for deletion; residual copies in backups, logs, fraud-prevention records, or delayed cleanup systems may remain for up to 90 days or longer only where legally required. Aggregated or de-identified information may be retained where it no longer identifies you.

8. Your choices and rights

You can use core logging offline, turn reminders off, deny notifications, export supported records, sign out, and request account deletion. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or obtain a copy of personal data, and to complain to a regulator. Contact support@nurtlina.app. We may need to verify your request.

9. Account deletion

In the app, open Settings → Account → Delete account & data. If you no longer have the app, use our external deletion page. Deleting Nurtlina does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as the app or law changes. We will update the date above and provide additional notice when required.

Nurtlina：宝宝喂养记录

隐私政策

生效及最后更新日期：2026 年 8 月 18 日

本政策说明您使用 Nurtlina Android 应用、网站及相关服务时，我们如何处理信息。隐私问题请联系 support@nurtlina.app。

1. 适用对象

Nurtlina 面向成年父母及照护者，不直接面向儿童，也不提供医疗服务。建议使用昵称而非宝宝法定姓名。

2. 处理的信息

照护记录：宝宝昵称、可选出生日期、喂养、尿布、睡眠、提醒反馈、设置及可选备注。核心记录保存在本机；登录并使用备份或同步时，所选记录和同步元数据会传至后端。

账号：使用 Google 登录时，我们可能接收用于认证、家庭空间、备份同步和删除账号所需的账号标识符、邮箱及显示名称。

购买：Google Play 与 RevenueCat 处理购买。我们接收提供和恢复 Pro 权益所需的商品、交易、权益及应用用户标识，不接收完整银行卡信息。

分析、诊断与广告：Firebase Analytics 可能接收功能使用、粗略喂养量区间、提醒互动、购买状态、语言及小组件等事件；Crashlytics 可能接收崩溃与设备/应用诊断；AdMob 可能处理设备标识符、IP 地址、广告互动及诊断数据。宝宝姓名和备注内容不会作为分析事件上传。

设备数据：应用在本机处理通知权限和设备时间。根据 Android 与 Google 备份设置，系统可能把应用数据纳入设备备份或迁移。

3. 使用目的

用于本地记录、汇总、提醒、小组件、导出、备份同步、账号保护、购买恢复、故障诊断、隐私安全的产品分析、向符合条件的免费用户展示广告，以及处理支持和隐私请求。

4. 共享及服务商

我们不出售宝宝照护记录。运营服务可能使用 Google Firebase、Google Play、Google AdMob、RevenueCat 以及 API/数据库基础设施服务商。服务商依其条款和隐私声明处理必要信息。法律要求或为保护用户与服务时，我们也可能依法披露。

5. 广告与选择

免费用户可能看到 Today 页横幅；Pro 用户和夜间模式不展示。法律要求时，我们会提供相应的广告或分析选择。宝宝照护记录不会用于广告定向。

6. 存储与安全

核心功能可离线使用；云端传输使用加密连接；同步记录仅限相应认证账号或家庭访问。服务商可能依据适用保障措施在您所在国家/地区以外处理数据。

7. 保留期限

本地数据保留至您删除、清除应用数据或卸载，但受 Android 备份机制影响。云端数据在账号有效期间保留。收到删除请求后，账号识别字段会被移除，关联云端记录会被删除或标记删除；备份、日志、防欺诈记录或延迟清理系统中的残余副本可能保留最多 90 天，法律另有要求的除外。无法再识别您的汇总或去标识数据可继续保留。

8. 您的权利

您可以离线使用核心记录、关闭提醒、拒绝通知、导出支持的数据、退出登录和删除账号。视所在地而定，您还可能享有访问、更正、删除、限制、反对、复制数据及向监管机构投诉的权利。提出请求时可能需要验证身份。

9. 删除账号

应用内进入 Settings → Account → Delete account & data；无法访问应用时使用外部删除页面。删除 Nurtlina 账号不会自动取消 Google Play 订阅。

10. 政策变更

应用或法律变化时我们可能更新本政策，并更新上述日期；法律要求时会另行通知。
