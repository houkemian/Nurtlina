Delete your account and data

Last updated: 18 August 2026

You can request deletion even if you have uninstalled Nurtlina or cannot access the app.

Request deletion by email
Send the request from the email address associated with your Nurtlina account. Do not include baby names, care notes, or other sensitive details.
Email deletion request

Delete inside the app

  1. Open Nurtlina.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Under Account, select Delete account & data.
  4. Review the warning and confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your Nurtlina account profile and authentication association.
  • Family membership or an owned family workspace, as applicable.
  • Cloud-synced baby profiles, feeding, diaper, and sleep records associated with an owned family.
  • Cloud sync cursors and active Nurtlina entitlement records associated with the account.

What may remain

Local records on a device do not disappear from every device merely because a cloud request is submitted. Clear Nurtlina app storage or uninstall it on devices you control after confirming any export you need. Residual copies in backups, security logs, fraud-prevention records, or delayed cleanup systems may remain for up to 90 days or longer where required by law. De-identified aggregate information may remain.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel separately in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Nurtlina. Cancellation stops future renewal but does not normally refund past charges.

Verification and timing

We may ask you to verify control of the account to protect it from unauthorized deletion. We aim to initiate verified requests promptly and complete production-system cleanup within 30 days, with residual backup cleanup within the retention period described above. We will explain any legally required exception.

Delete only certain data

You can delete individual care records in the app without deleting your account. For another access, correction, export, or deletion request, email support@muyestudio.net.
